HeyGen

HeyGen · Video Generation · Turn a script into an on-camera avatar video, no camera or actor needed.

HeyGen is a video platform built around on-camera AI avatars: you supply a script, and it generates a video of a presenter speaking it in a synthesized voice.

That makes it a different kind of video generation than a text-to-video motion tool — the output is a talking-head style video meant for explaining, presenting, or narrating, not a cinematic scene.
