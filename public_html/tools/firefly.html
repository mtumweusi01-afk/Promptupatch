Adobe Firefly

Adobe · Image Generation · Commercially-safe image generation, Photoshop-native.

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's image generation tool, trained with commercial licensing in mind and built into Photoshop and other Creative Cloud apps.

It's often chosen when generated images need to be used commercially with fewer licensing concerns.
