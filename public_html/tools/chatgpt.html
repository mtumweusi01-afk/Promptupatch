ChatGPT

OpenAI · Chat & Writing · General-purpose chat, fast and flexible.

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational assistant, built for everyday writing, explaining, and problem-solving through natural back-and-forth chat.

It's widely used as a general starting point for drafting, brainstorming, and quick research, with support for images, files, and voice alongside plain text.
