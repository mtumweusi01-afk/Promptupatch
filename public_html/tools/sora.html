Sora

OpenAI · Video Generation · High-fidelity text-to-video generation.

Sora is OpenAI's text-to-video model, built to generate longer, higher-fidelity video clips from a written scene description.

It's aimed at users who need more realistic motion and cinematography than quick short-form generators typically produce.

What people use Sora for

  • realistic video
  • film
  • scene
  • footage

Alternatives in Video Generation
