Gemini

Google · Chat & Writing · Google-integrated chat with multimodal input.

Gemini is Google's assistant, built to work naturally with images, text, and other media in the same conversation.

Its closest advantage is how tightly it ties into the rest of Google's ecosystem, like Gmail, Docs, and Search.
