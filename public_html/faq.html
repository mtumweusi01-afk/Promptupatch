Frequently asked questions

How do you decide which tool to recommend?

First we run your prompt against a keyword list for each category, which catches the obvious cases instantly. If it's ambiguous, we ask an AI model to make the call. Either way you end up with one matched tool and a preview answer on the same page.

What's the deal with credits?

Each prompt costs 1 credit, and you start with 10 free ones. Your balance always shows above the prompt box, so nothing sneaks up on you. Run out and you can grab a pack from the Pricing page. Packs never expire.

Who actually writes the preview answer?

Our own AI does, not the matched tool. It's meant to give you a head start, not replace the real thing. If you want the matched tool's actual output, use the 'Open in tool' button under the answer.

Does the prompt really carry over when I open a tool?

For tools that support it, like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Suno, yes. It lands right in their input box. Some tools don't allow that, so for those we copy your prompt to your clipboard and open the tool. One paste and you're going.

How do payments work?

Pick a plan on the Pricing page and you'll land on checkout. We take USDT on the Tron network (TRC20). Send the amount shown, drop in your transaction ID, and we'll add your credits once it clears.

Do I need accounts with all these tools?

Yes, wherever the tool itself requires one. We don't create accounts or hold logins for you. We just get you and your prompt to the right door. You sign in like normal once you're there.

What happens to my prompts?

They're used to figure out the right tool and generate your preview, and that's it. We don't build up a database of what you've typed.

Can I suggest a tool you're missing?

Please do. The Contact page is the way. Bonus points if the tool supports prefilled prompts through its URL, because those make the whole hand-off smoother.
