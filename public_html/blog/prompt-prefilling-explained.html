How prompt hand-off actually works

How it works · Jun 3, 2026

A handful of AI products accept a query parameter in their URL that pre-fills the input box the moment the page loads. That's what lets PromptuPatch open a tool with your text already sitting there, ready to send.

Plenty of tools don't offer that. Either the product wasn't built for it, or it lives behind an app rather than a web page. In those cases, we copy your prompt to your clipboard automatically and open the tool, so all that's left is one paste.

It's a small mechanical difference, but it's the whole trick behind a one-click router.
