Why we price in credits, not seats

How it works · Jul 1, 2026

Every prompt you run has a real cost behind it — model inference isn't free, and it scales with usage, not with the number of accounts. Credit pricing lines up what you pay with what you use.

For light users, that's strictly better than a subscription: your credits never expire, so an occasional burst of use doesn't require a monthly commitment you'll mostly waste.

For heavy users, packs get cheaper per credit as they grow, and the Unlimited tier exists for anyone whose usage makes counting pointless.

The 10 free credits every account starts with aren't a trial timer — they're enough to genuinely evaluate whether the routing and previews are useful to you before spending anything.
