Why 'best AI tool' is the wrong question

Guide · Jun 12, 2026

People ask us all the time which AI tool is the best. Honestly? Wrong question. It's like asking which kitchen knife is best. A chef's knife, a paring knife, and a bread knife all cut things, but nobody peels an apple with a bread knife.

AI tools work the same way. A model tuned for careful long-form reasoning isn't the right pick for generating a stylized illustration, and a video generator isn't going to write you a clean cover letter.

The fastest way to get good output is matching the shape of your request to the shape of the tool, not defaulting to whichever tool you already have a tab open for.
